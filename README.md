# 🩺 Medkit Clicker

**A cozy pixel-art game about building your very own hospital, one tap at a time.**

Tap the medkit to earn medkits. Spend them on medical supplies, from simple bandages all the way up to a DNA lab, that keep earning medkits for you. Watch your little clinic grow into a grand medical center, collect achievements, decorate your rooms, and relax to a lo-fi soundtrack that fits the mood.

Medkit Clicker is available on Android phones & tablets, ~~iPhone & iPad,~~ and in your web browser. It was created by Dustin Dwayne Diaz.

---

## What is it like to play?

Medkit Clicker is an "idle" game, which means it's easy to pick up and just as happy to run quietly in the background. Every tap on the medkit earns medkits. The more supplies you buy, the more you earn on your own, even while you're away. Your team keeps working for up to 4 hours after you close the game, so you'll always come back to a busier hospital.

There's no rush and no pressure. Play for a minute or an hour. Your hospital will be waiting for you.

## What you can do

### 🏥 Grow your hospital

Start with a single medkit and build up your supplies. There are 13 to collect, including bandages, gloves, IV bags, microscopes and a DNA lab. Each one you buy makes the next a little pricier, and every supply doubles its output at 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100 owned, so even the humble bandage stays worth upgrading.

### 🚑 Handle emergencies

Life at a hospital is never dull. Cars drive past and visitors walk through the door. When an ambulance pulls up out front, tap it to admit the patient and get a big burst of medkits. Now and then a winged heart flutters past the medkit too: catch it for a bonus or a tap frenzy.

### 🌟 Start fresh with Rebirth

Ready for a new beginning? Rebirth lets you start a brand-new run in exchange for a Natal Gem and a small permanent healing boost. As you rebirth, your hospital transforms too, growing through 10 different looks, from a humble little clinic to the magnificent Grand Haven Medical Center.

### 🏆 Collect achievements

There are 199 achievements to discover, from your very first "First Aid" all the way to earning a truly absurd number of medkits. Each comes with a progress bar so you can see how close you are, and a few are secret.

### 🪙 Three kinds of rewards

Keep an eye on the top-right corner of the screen:

- **Medkits** are your main currency, earned by tapping and from your supplies.
- **Care Coins** are earned from achievements and spent on special boosts.
- **Natal Gems** are earned each time you rebirth.

### ⚡ Power-ups and boosts

Spend your Care Coins in the shop on 13 different boosts, like tripling every tap for 5 minutes, or instantly gaining two hours' worth of medkits.

### 🛋️ Decorate My Room

Make the hospital your own! Furnish rooms such as the Reception, Lab, Pharmacy and Children's Ward with 26 pixel-art items, some of them animated. You unlock new decorations just by playing, and every item you place gives you a small permanent bonus. Assign the nurses and the doctor to a room too: drag them in and they get to work.

### 🤒 Treat sick patients with mini-games

Every so often, a sick patient shows up in your room, each time looking a little different. Help them with one of fourteen quick mini-games:

- Injection timing
- CPR rhythm
- Bandage race
- Pulse counting
- Pill sorting
- X-ray
- Germ busting
- Breathing exercises
- Memory pairs
- Healthy shopping (catch the good food)
- Odd colour out
- Find it
- Blood-cell maze
- IV pipe puzzle

Each one rewards you with medkits and coins.

### 📅 Every day, every step

Open the game each day for a daily gift that grows over a 7-day streak. On Android you can also turn on walking rewards: your phone counts your steps, and every 500 m and every kilometre (up to 8 km a day) pays out in the game. Android phones can add a home-screen widget that shows your medkits at a glance.

### 🎁 Redeem codes and rewards

Discover special rewards by redeeming codes throughout your journey. You never know what little surprises might be waiting for you!

## 🎵 Music and sound

Medkit Clicker comes with its own original soundtrack: five relaxing lo-fi tunes made just for the game.

- _Waiting Room_
- _Morning Rounds_
- _Night Shift_
- _Visiting Hours_ (a gentle waltz)
- _Recovery Room_

There's a built-in music player where you can play, pause, skip tracks and jump around within a song. Sound effects have their own volume control, and on phones you can turn vibration feedback on or off.

---

## 🎨 Make it yours

- **Light and dark themes** to suit your eyes and your mood.
- **Backgrounds** for every taste: patterns, animated scenes, a galaxy for dark mode, or your own photo, GIF or short video clip.
- **Swipe between scenes:** My Room sits next to the medkit, and the outside of the hospital sits next to the shop, each drawn as a full-width pixel scene.
- **Works on any screen:** on tablets and computers the shop sits beside the medkit, and on phones it moves below it.

---

## 💾 Your progress is safe

The game saves automatically every few seconds and whenever you leave the app. You can also back up your progress to a file of your choice, or restore it later, so you never have to lose your hospital.

## 💙 The story behind the game

Medkit Clicker started as a gift. Dustin made it for Jasmine Sison Taboy, a nursing student, to help her unwind during stressful times. That's why the game is all about nursing and caring for others, and why there's a hidden rewards section waiting to be found.
